Side effects

One clear gain and one clear strain. Aquifers recovered as seepage from canals, tanks and paddy refilled them. Hyderabad's supply did not grow while the city did.

How the model works. The ≈ figures are an indicative water balance, not an official account. Both years use one normal-rainfall year (about 905 mm over 1.12 lakh km², roughly 3,580 TMC). Groundwater totals are anchored to CGWB assessments; river draws, tank storage and domestic supply are set to be consistent with project data and allocations. River draws include water that originates upstream in Maharashtra and Karnataka. Return seepage is shown as its own source so the flow chart has no loops. Conversions: 1 BCM = 35.31 TMC; 1 MLD ≈ 0.0129 TMC/yr.
